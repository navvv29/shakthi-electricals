Our Story

One Man.
Decades of Mastery.

For over 25 years, a single craftsman has been the person Edavannapara turns to when electrical devices stop working. What began as a humble repair bench has grown into the community's most trusted name in electrical repair.

From a ceiling fan that won't spin to an industrial motor that needs rewinding — every device that enters this shop leaves working like new. That's not just a promise, it's a legacy built on decades of dedication.

No middlemen, no showroom overhead — just one skilled pair of hands, honest pricing, and a deep understanding of how every electrical device works.
